Welcome to Principles of One Health

WFA 4863/6863 · Fall 2026 · Lectures Mon & Wed 12:00–12:50 · Lab Wed 2:00–3:50

One planet, One system, One health.

Everything in health is connected — forests to fevers, markets to landscapes, policy to pathogens. This course teaches you to see the connections and trace the mechanisms: how human, animal, and environmental health shape each other, and where we could intervene for the benefit of people. You will learn to think in systems!

Meet your instructor

Dr. Manuel Ruiz Aravena. I am a veterinarian and disease ecologist. My research focuses on how pathogens circulate in wildlife and cross into new species, including humans.

How this course works, in one minute

The semester runs in three arcs — Foundations (Weeks 1–5), Zoonotic Interfaces (Weeks 6–11), and Making It Real (Weeks 12–15) — with the same rhythm every week: companion readings before class, discussion-driven sessions, and a Wednesday lab where teams map real systems. The full picture — teaching philosophy, how labs and grading work, what to expect — is on How this course works.

This site is your course companion. Everything official — the syllabus, assignment submissions, quizzes, discussions, grades, and announcements — lives in Canvas.
