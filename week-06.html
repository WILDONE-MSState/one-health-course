Pathogen Spillover & Wildlife Reservoirs

Roughly three of every four emerging infectious diseases begin in animals. This week we follow a pathogen across the species barrier — and learn to read the ecology of the reservoirs it comes from.

By Friday you can…

  1. Define spillover, reservoir host, and amplifier host with real examples
  2. Map the stages of the spillover pathway for a named outbreak
  3. Explain how reservoir ecology shapes shedding and exposure risk
  4. Navigate the major One Health data sources for your team's review topic
